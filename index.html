Bystander: what to do

  1. Stay calm and make sure the area is safe from traffic.
  2. Call the ambulance first, then police.
  3. Do not remove the helmet unless the rider is not breathing.
  4. Do not move the rider unless there is immediate danger.
  5. Alert family using the SMS button above.
